# Visual assets and attribution

ProtoMind Lab's agent atlas, geometric brand symbol, wordmarks, and application icon were created for this project. The PNG and ICNS files are derivatives of those original assets. The atlas depicts synthetic prey, a predator, and resource clusters; its shading is visual only and does not add a depth coordinate to the simulation. No stock imagery or external fonts are bundled.

These original assets are supplied under the repository's MIT license. No trademark registration or exclusive rights are claimed. The model weights are excluded and retain a separate license.

The bundled interface uses PixiJS 8.17.0 (MIT) and Lucide 0.468.0 (ISC, including Feather MIT portions). Required upstream notices for these and their runtime dependencies are preserved in [third-party notices](../web/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt).
