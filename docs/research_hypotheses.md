# Research hypotheses and estimands

These are prospective comparisons, not established findings. The primary unit of replication is an independent world seed, with paired conditions sharing initialization and ecological parameters.

1. **H1, objective selection:** Relative to mechanistic A, real LFM3 condition B may change mean survival time and exposure under the frozen predator scenario. The null is no paired difference. Report food collection and alignment to detect tradeoffs.
2. **H2, individual memory:** C versus B isolates retrieval of the agent's own earlier records. The primary estimand is paired change in mean survival time; secondary outcomes are food collection, exposure, decision validity, and token cost. SQLite retrieval is not neural learning.
3. **H3, information control:** D versus C asks whether informative memory content matters beyond having memory-shaped prompt material. D scrambles stored values while preserving record shape. Token counts are measured because exact prompt-token matching is not guaranteed.
4. **H4, collective interaction:** Setting social strength to zero in mechanistic A removes separation, alignment, and cohesion while retaining predator avoidance and individual objectives. Compare polarization, fragmentation, exposure, and survival on paired seeds. A null or reversed effect is possible.
5. **H5, ecology:** Predator perception, speed, prey sensing, resource count, group size, and decision interval may alter encounter and survival distributions. Sensitivity scans are descriptive and cannot be used to re-tune the frozen evaluation configuration after examining LFM3 outcomes.

The primary endpoint is mean survival time censored at the episode limit. Survival curves and final survival fractions accompany it. Paired mean differences and empirical ranges are always reported; deterministic bootstrap 95% intervals are generated only for at least five paired seeds. Those intervals describe simulation-seed variability, not biological-population uncertainty. No significance or superiority claim should follow from a short pilot.
